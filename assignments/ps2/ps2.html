0.1 Overview

This assignment has two parts:

  1. Research abstract + DAG (your proposed study)
  2. Published paper DAG (existing study in a general-interest or top field journal)

For each part, you will:

  • State a causal research question
  • Provide a DAG (rendered in R or another tool)
  • List all paths between treatment and outcome (distinguish causal vs. non-causal/backdoor, collider, etc.)
  • List all assumptions implied by the DAG and identification claim
  • Identify two plausible threats to identification and provide one diagnostic per threat
